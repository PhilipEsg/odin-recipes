Curry Recipe


Home page

Description

Simple beef Japanese curry recipe.

Ingredient List

  • 1 cup of chopped yellow onions
  • 3 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 2 cups of chopped carrots
  • 2 lbs of beef chuck
  • 1 box of S&L curry roux

Optional

  • 2 tbsp instant coffee powder
  • 3 squares of dark chocolate
  • 1 tbsp of honey
